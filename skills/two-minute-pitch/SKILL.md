Write a pitch that works spoken aloud. The Velocity Pitch Competition has given each team's presenter two minutes and no slides, so the words must do all the work. Check the current format at https://www.velocityincubator.com/programs-events/vpc.

Input: $ARGUMENTS. If you lack traction facts, ask. Never invent numbers, customers or quotes.

## Structure for 2 minutes (about 280 spoken words)

1. **Open with a person and a problem (20 s).** A specific scene, not a market statistic.
2. **What you built (20 s).** What it does, in words anyone can picture. No "platform" or "solution".
3. **Why it matters (20 s).** Who pays, how much, and a bottom-up sense of how big this could be.
4. **Proof (30 s).** Your strongest real evidence: paying customers, pilots, signed letters, or the number of conversations and what you learned. Be exact, and call LOIs non-binding if asked.
5. **Why you (15 s).** The unfair insight or history that makes this team the right one.
6. **The ask (15 s).** One specific thing: an intro, a pilot partner, a cheque. Repeat the company name.

## Rules

- Write for the ear: short sentences, words you'd actually say, and only numbers people can hold in their heads.
- One idea per sentence, and cut anything the listener won't remember tomorrow.
- Show the word count and estimated time, at 140 words per minute. Leave room for pauses.

## Feedback mode

For an existing script, give timing per section, then the one thing the listener would remember, the first place they'd stop listening, jargon to remove, and any claim a judge would challenge. Prepare answers to the five hardest likely questions.

## IP check

Before a public pitch, confirm that nothing explains how an unprotected invention works. If it might, use the `ip-readiness` skill first.

## Deliver

Give the full version, plus a 30-second version and a one-sentence version. Tell the founder to rehearse aloud with a timer. Past pitches are on https://www.youtube.com/@uwvelocity.
