name: pilot-design
description: Design a paid pilot with measurable success criteria and a path to a contract. Use when a customer is willing to try the product, when a founder is scoping a proof of concept, or when turning a letter of intent into a real deployment.
---

Pilots often stall because nobody agreed what success means. Design one that ends in a clear decision.

## Gather

Who owns the problem, who owns the budget, who could block this (IT, privacy, legal, procurement, clinical leads), what success would mean to each, and the baseline today. If nobody has measured the baseline, measuring it becomes step one.

## Design

Produce a one-page pilot plan:

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Problem and baseline** | The number today, and its source |
| **Scope** | The smallest setting that proves value: one site, team or workflow |
| **Duration** | As short as the measurement allows |
| **Success criteria** | 2-3 measurable thresholds agreed up front, plus who judges them |
| **Failure criteria** | What stops the pilot early, including safety concerns |
| **Fee** | Ask for one. Even a small fee proves budget exists and gets the pilot taken seriously. |
| **Responsibilities** | A named person on each side, and their time commitment |
| **Data** | What's collected, where it's stored, and who owns it |
| **Decision** | A dated meeting and a pre-agreed outcome: buy at $X, extend, or stop |

## Check for stalls

Flag these, and suggest a fix for each: no economic buyer involved, success defined as "see if we like it", no end date, free with no commitment, or dependence on an integration that isn't built.

In regulated settings (health, finance, education), list the approvals and when they must start. Suggest legal and privacy advice. Don't claim that a product complies with anything unless the founder can show evidence.

## Hand-offs

- Record intent with `loi-drafter`, using the `pilot` or `health-pilot` type.
- A signed pilot agreement needs a contract, not a letter. Suggest a lawyer's review, and ask Velocity for introductions to service providers.
- Afterward, write up results with real numbers for `fundraise-prep`.
