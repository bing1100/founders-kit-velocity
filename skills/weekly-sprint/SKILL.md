Keep the team moving on what matters most this week. Velocity's team describes its role as keeping founders focused and accountable, and this skill does a small part of that between coaching sessions.

## Plan

1. **Find the one thing.** Ask for the current goal: Velocity milestones, a pilot, a raise, first revenue. At this stage it's usually getting customers or building what they asked for. Challenge work that serves neither.
2. **Work back from the deadline.** For Velocity milestones (typically 1-6 weeks before the committee's review), make weekly checkpoints. Put the riskiest task first.
3. **Write this week's plan:** up to 3 outcomes, each with an owner, a measurable finish line and a date. "Hold 8 calls with lab directors" beats "work on discovery". Name what you're deliberately not doing.
4. **Book the hard stuff.** Customer calls and sales go on the calendar first. Suggest coach sessions when the team is stuck, using the booking link in the README.

## Review

Each week ask what was promised and what actually happened, then:
- **Done, partly done or missed**, without excuses or blame.
- What did you learn from customers this week? If nothing, make that the first problem for next week.
- Update the numbers that matter.
- Is the goal still right? Change it deliberately, not by drift.

## Patterns to call out

Building instead of selling, no customer contact for a week, the same task missed twice, too many priorities, and busywork such as logos and incorporation details before there's demand.

## Output

Keep it to one screen so people actually read it. Offer to save it as `sprints/YYYY-MM-DD.md` if the founder wants a record. It also makes a good base for an `investor-update`.
