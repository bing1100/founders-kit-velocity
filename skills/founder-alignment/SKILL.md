description: Run a structured conversation between cofounders on roles, equity, vesting, commitment and conflict, and capture the founder story for applications. Use when forming a founding team, splitting equity, handling cofounder tension, or answering how the founders know each other.
---

Most of this is about surfacing disagreements before they become expensive. Stay neutral. If there's a conflict, hear each founder's view and don't take sides. Suggest a professional mediator or lawyer if things are serious. This is not legal or tax advice.

## Ask each founder separately first

Have them write answers independently, then compare. Differences are the point.
- **Commitment:** full-time when? What would make you leave? What's your personal runway? Velocity requires all founders to make the startup their primary focus.
- **Ambition:** what outcome would make you happy in 5 years? Would you sell for $10M?
- **Roles:** who decides on product, money, hiring? What happens when you disagree?
- **Equity:** what's fair, and why? What if one person's contribution changes?
- **Money:** salaries, personal investment, how long you can go unpaid?
- **Exit from the team:** what happens if someone leaves after 6 months?
- **Values:** what wouldn't you do to win?

## Agreements to get in writing

Vesting (four years with a one-year cliff is a common default), what happens to unvested and vested shares when someone leaves, IP assignment to the company, decision rights, deadlock resolution, and outside work. Recommend a lawyer drafts the founder agreement. Velocity can guide teams through incorporation.

## Founder story for applications

Velocity asks how founders know each other and for how long, and teams of 2-4 have better outcomes in its experience. Draft a short answer from true examples of building together, working under pressure, resolving conflict and helping each other grow. "We studied the same program" is weak. If the team hasn't been tested, suggest a real project together and be honest about it.

## Output

A comparison of where founders agree and differ, the top 3 conversations to have this week, a term summary for a lawyer, and the founder story draft.
