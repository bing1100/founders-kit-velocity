# Email template: asking for a letter of intent

Works with `tools/automated-outreach/send_outreach.py`. Send it only to people you have already spoken to. Pass `--template` and your CSV, and attach or link the draft yourself after they reply: the sender sends plain text with no attachments.

**Subject:** Following up on {{Topic}}: short letter of intent?

---

Hi {{FirstName}},

Thanks for talking with me about {{Topic}}. You said {{WhatTheySaid}}, and that has shaped what we're building.

Would you be willing to sign a short, non-binding letter of intent saying that {{Organization}} would like to {{Ask}} if we can deliver? It doesn't commit you to anything. It helps us show investors and grant reviewers that real organizations want this.

I've drafted one from our conversation, so you'd only need to correct anything I got wrong. Can I send it over?

Best,
{{SenderName}}

If you'd prefer not to hear from me again, just reply and let me know.

---
