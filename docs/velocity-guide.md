## At a glance

- University of Waterloo's flagship entrepreneurship program, running since 2008. It reports helping the founders of 500+ startups reach a combined enterprise value of $40B+.
- **Free, with no equity taken.** Velocity says it asks founders only to stay focused and aim high. ([How we help](https://www.velocityincubator.com/how-we-help))
- Two audiences:
  - **Students, researchers, faculty and staff** at UW, through free programs at any stage, with or without an idea. ([Students](https://www.velocityincubator.com/students))
  - **Full-time founders** in the incubator, from Waterloo or anywhere, if they meet the program requirements. ([Apply](https://www.velocityincubator.com/apply))
- The incubator is at the **Innovation Arena, 280 Joseph Street, Kitchener**.

## The incubator: who gets in

Summarized from the [Apply FAQ](https://www.velocityincubator.com/apply):

| Question | Velocity's answer, summarized |
|---|---|
| What kinds of startups? | Any industry, technology or business model |
| Solo founders or big teams? | Yes, but teams of 2-4 founders do better in its experience |
| Students, professors, dropouts, high schoolers, established professionals? | Yes, if the startup is your primary focus |
| Non-technical founders? | Yes. The more complex the product, the more Velocity will push you to find a technical partner. |
| Founders in Canada on a visa? | Yes. Startup visas take a long time, and a lapsed visa can endanger the business, so work with an immigration lawyer. |
| **Part-time founders?** | **No.** Every founder must give the business undivided attention. Velocity suggests other organizations for this stage, and applying once that changes. |
| Not incorporated yet? | Fine. It's quick and inexpensive, and Velocity can guide you. |
| Can't move to Kitchener-Waterloo? | **Only if you can work from Velocity at least 1 day a week** (ideally 5-7) for the first 3 months, and join coaching and programming |
| Already raised money, or in other accelerators? | Yes |
| Idea stage? | Sometimes. Prior execution carries a lot of weight, but so do ambition, customer obsession and founder-market fit. |
| Repeat founders, or years into the business? | Yes |
| Do I need to be a UW alum? | No ([How we help](https://www.velocityincubator.com/how-we-help)) |
| How strict are the rules? | Every rule has exceptions, but each rule exists for a reason |
| What businesses can't it support? | As part of a university, it can't support businesses such as gambling, firearms or illicit substances |

## How to apply

Apply at **[forms.fillout.com/t/ocQRRAT8bLus](https://forms.fillout.com/t/ocQRRAT8bLus)** (linked from the [Apply page](https://www.velocityincubator.com/apply)).

- Applications are open **all year**.
- New teams are onboarded **every two months**: January, March, May, July, September and November.
- About **10%** are accepted.
- You should hear back within a couple of weeks.

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| 1. You fill in the application | ~30 min |
| 2. Selection committee reviews it | 1-2 weeks |
| 3. Virtual interview | 30 min |
| 4. Committee assigns milestones based on the interview | 1-2 weeks |
| 5. **You complete the milestones** | 1-6 weeks |
| 6. Second virtual meeting to review the milestones | 30 min |

From application to decision, plan for roughly **one to three months**, most of it spent on your milestones. The `weekly-sprint` skill helps plan them.

## Writing a strong application

Velocity's application tips focus on four things. Each tip on the Apply page has a great, an average and a disappointing example, so read them there. Summarized:

1. **Show your customer discovery in numbers.** It gets very high emphasis. Say how many real conversations you've had, when, with whom and how you reached them. Speaking only to people you know isn't enough. Surveys are less compelling than conversations and need hundreds of responses at minimum.
   → Kit: [`velocity-check discovery`](../tools/velocity-check/), skill `customer-discovery`
2. **Explain how the founders know each other, and for how long.** Show you've built something together, been under pressure, handled conflict and helped each other grow. "We studied the same program" doesn't answer it.
   → Skill `founder-alignment`
3. **No buzzwords or jargon.** If 10 words will do, don't use 50, and make it understandable to children and seniors. "Platform" and "solution" don't paint a picture. If reviewers can't picture your product, the application suffers.
   → Kit: [`velocity-check lint`](../tools/velocity-check/)
4. **Size the market bottom-up.** Market-report totals and growth rates aren't compelling on their own. Explain how many potential customers exist, how you segment them, what you'll charge, and why you'll convert more than anyone else. Velocity says it will do the multiplying.
   → Kit: [`velocity-check market`](../tools/velocity-check/), skill `market-sizing`

Velocity also mentions "yellow flags" it has learned to notice. The tips above are its guidance on avoiding them.

## If you get in

- Work from Velocity at least **1 day a week (ideally 5-7) for the first 3 months**, so you join the community and the team learns how to help you.
- Meet coaches and take part in programming. Early-stage founders can expect a couple of hours of programming a week, which becomes ad hoc as the company grows.
- There are **no formal cohorts**. You'll overlap with founders who joined in the six months before and after you.
- **Engagement pays off.** Velocity says founders who visit the desk, use Slack and ask for help get the most value, and are considered first for funding, media and other opportunities.
- Introductions to investors and customers must be **earned**.
- No fixed length of stay, though established companies may be asked to find their own space when Velocity is full. Desks are capped at **10 per company**, and it isn't a coworking space.
- Social events are founder-run, and most chat happens on a shared Slack.

## Why applications get rejected

Velocity lists these common reasons ([Apply](https://www.velocityincubator.com/apply)):
- incomplete or thin application
- a business a university can't support
- not able to meet program requirements
- Velocity lacks the experience or resources to help
- a competing team already in the space (it usually checks with one or both companies)
- not enough proof the problem is big enough
- not enough proof the customer would buy
- unclear what your unique insight is

Most of the time there's no single reason. Velocity points to Y Combinator's explanation: [ycombinator.com/whynot](https://www.ycombinator.com/whynot). **Reapplying is encouraged** once your company has changed. Lead with what changed.

## Student programs FAQ

Summarized from the [Students page](https://www.velocityincubator.com/students):

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does it cost money or equity? | No. It's funded by the university and free. |
| Only for undergrads? | No: grad students, faculty and staff too, including researchers commercializing their work |
| Do I need an idea, a prototype, a cofounder or coding skills? | No |
| Can I also join other programs (YC, Communitech and others)? | Yes. Velocity sees itself as your home base, and will help you pursue other programs and review offers with you. |
| How do I start? | Fill in the [Get started form](https://forms.fillout.com/t/5ZQrB5NE7Pus), request to join the Discord, and join the [mailing list](https://www.velocityincubator.com/newsletter-subscribe) |

**1:1 coaching:** browse the [Velocity Expert Community](https://www.velocityincubator.com/velocity-expert-community). Experts cover AI, B2B sales, business development, consumer and retail, edtech, fintech, fundraising and grants, hardware, health, marketing, idea validation, product development and software. All sessions are booked through [one form](https://form.fillout.com/t/5ZQrB5NE7Pus). Cancel at least 24 hours ahead: missing more than two sessions without notice ends your eligibility.

## Velocity Fund

[velocity.fund](https://velocity.fund/) is a pre-seed fund. Its offices are at the entrance to the incubator, it's anchored by the UW Endowment, and it is run by two general partners, both UW alumni who built companies at Velocity. It's separate from the free incubator: Velocity introduces founders to it, but funding isn't automatic. Its site states:
- **Cheques:** scout cheques of $25K. Core cheques are $100K-$1M on the home page and $150K-$1M on the How We Help page.
- **Terms:** it leads rounds and takes 5-7.5% ownership.
- **Process:** a decision within two weeks of the first meeting, on the second Thursday after it, with diligence questions shared upfront.
- **Thesis:** about 70% of its portfolio is Waterloo founders, and about 30% is technical teams from elsewhere with early signal.
- **Contact:** hello@velocity.fund. Read the [process](https://velocity.fund/how-we-help.html).
